# Do / Don't

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (brand voice)
> **Purpose:** Fast self-check pairs — scan before any copy goes out.

- **Do** lead with the point; **don't** warm up with throat-clearing ("I just wanted to reach out…").
- **Do** name the price plainly; **don't** bury or apologise for it.
- **Do** explain one *why*; **don't** write a lecture.
- **Do** be honest about trade-offs and risk; **don't** over-promise to win the line.
- **Do** use short sentences and white space; **don't** stack clauses.
- **Do** sound like a person ("I'll send this Friday"); **don't** sound like a portal ("Your request has been received").
- **Do** gloss any unavoidable jargon in one line; **don't** assume the reader is technical.
- **Do** end with a single clear next step; **don't** offer five options and no recommendation.
- **Do** keep it warm and specific; **don't** reach for hype or emoji.

If a sentence fails two of these, rewrite it. If the *same* failure recurs across runs, fix [`tone.md`](tone.md) or [`vocabulary.md`](vocabulary.md), not just the sentence (edit-source).
