# Tone of Voice

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (brand voice)
> **Purpose:** How Jamie Nisbet sounds in writing — the attributes, the register, and before/after examples every piece of copy follows.

The brand is **Jamie Nisbet** — a person, not a corporation. The voice is the written continuation of an in-person conversation with a sharp, friendly engineer who explains things clearly. Primary language: **English** (UK/EU spelling). Write to one reader — "you".

## The five attributes
1. **Friendly** — warm, human, first-person. "I'll…", "let's…". Never stiff or corporate.
2. **Informative** — explain the *why*, briefly. Teach a little; leave the reader smarter.
3. **Expert** — quiet confidence from specifics, not adjectives. Show the reasoning, not the résumé.
4. **Approachable** — plain words, short sentences. No jargon without a one-line gloss.
5. **Trustworthy** — honest about trade-offs, cost, and what you don't know. Under-promise.

The throughline: **friendly + informative builds trust in the expertise and confidence that working with Jamie is easy.** When two attributes conflict, keep *clarity* and *honesty*.

## Register
- Conversational but precise. Contractions yes ("you'll", "it's").
- Confident, not boastful. Specific, not vague. Concise, not curt.
- Active voice, present tense. Lead with the point.
- A little personality is good; emoji and hype are not.

## Before / after
- ❌ "We leverage cutting-edge AI solutions to drive synergistic outcomes."
  ✅ "I build the AI feature, wire it into what you already use, and show you it working."
- ❌ "Pursuant to our discussion, please find attached the proposal."
  ✅ "Here's the proposal we talked about — the scope and price are on page 2."
- ❌ "Unfortunately, we are unable to accommodate that timeline."
  ✅ "That timeline's tight — here's what I can deliver by then, and what would slip."
- ❌ "Our pricing is highly competitive."
  ✅ "It's €120/hour. For this scope that's about €X — here's what drives it."
