# Vocabulary

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (brand voice)
> **Purpose:** Preferred terms, how we name things, and words to avoid — so copy is consistent and jargon-free.

## Naming
- The business is **Jamie Nisbet** (personal name; no "Studios", "Agency", "Ltd" in the brand name).
- Refer to Jamie in the **first person** in client-facing copy ("I", "me"); third person only in formal/legal documents ("Jamie Nisbet", "the Consultant").
- Describe services by **outcome**, not product names: "a landing page", "an AI feature", "an automation" — not branded SKUs.

## Prefer → avoid
| Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|
| build, ship, wire up | leverage, utilise, synergise |
| help, work with you | solutions, offerings |
| AI feature / automation | AI-powered next-gen platform |
| price, cost | investment (as a euphemism for price) |
| problem, goal | pain point, value-add |
| clear, simple | seamless, frictionless, turnkey |
| I'll / let's | we are pleased to / kindly |

## Words to avoid entirely
Corporate filler (cutting-edge, world-class, best-in-class, robust, bespoke-as-filler), hype (revolutionary, game-changing), and trust-eroding hedges (try to, hopefully, should be fine).

## Numbers, currency, dates
- Currency: **€** before the figure, no space — €120, €1,500. Default currency EUR.
- Rate shorthand: **€120/hour**.
- Dates: `15 June 2026` in prose; `2026-06-15` in filenames and data.
- English (UK/EU) spelling: organise, colour, prioritise.
