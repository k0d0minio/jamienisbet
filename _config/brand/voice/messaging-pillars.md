# Messaging Pillars

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (brand voice)
> **Purpose:** The core messages every deliverable reinforces, so the whole business says one consistent thing.

Every proposal, website page, and email should land at least one of these. They are *what* we say; [`tone.md`](tone.md) is *how*.

1. **A senior engineer, working directly with you.** No agency layers, no account managers — you talk to the person building it. *(trust + ease)*
2. **AI that ships and earns its keep.** Practical AI and software wired into what you already use, measured against your outcome — not a demo. *(expertise + value)*
3. **Clear scope, clear price, no surprises.** You know what you're getting and what it costs before we start; changes are a conversation, not a hidden line item. *(trust)*
4. **Small shop, serious delivery.** One person you can reach, with contractors on standby for bigger builds — so you get responsiveness *and* capacity. *(ease + capability)*

## Using them
- A website hero leads with Pillar 1 or 2; a proposal cover reinforces Pillar 3.
- Don't cram all four into one paragraph — pick the one that fits the moment.
- If a claim can't be traced to a pillar, question whether it belongs.
