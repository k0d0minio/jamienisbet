# Rates & Pricing

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (business fact). Decision-support; the tax treatment of these
> figures (IVA, retention) must be confirmed with a contabilista — see
> [`_config/conventions/governance.md`](../conventions/governance.md).

## Standard rate
- **€120 / hour** — the standard rate for software / AI consulting.
- Project work is estimated in hours against this rate, then presented as a fixed price.

## Flex levers (used in negotiation — see [`workspaces/proposals/`](../../workspaces/proposals/))
- **Monthly retainer:** a slight hourly reduction in exchange for committed, recurring hours.
- **Commission / incentive:** open to a reduced hourly rate in exchange for commission or
  outcome-based upside (revenue share, success fee) — price the upside, not just the hours.
- **Floor:** the rate must never drop below the floor configured in
  `workspaces/proposals/setup/` (kept there, not published here).

## Pricing approach
- **Tiered good / better / best by default** on every quote — anchor with three options.
- **Value-based:** price against the client's upside where it can be estimated, not just hours.

## Affiliate / landing-page offer (see [`workspaces/lead-generation/`](../../workspaces/lead-generation/) `03_affiliate_program`)
- Sellers may quote a **landing page + contact form** freely, **minimum €200**, without approval.
- Anything more complex requires Jamie's sign-off.
- Affiliate commission: **10%**, paid on payment received from the client.

> These are business facts set by Jamie, not tax advice. VAT/IVA handling depends on the
> (not-yet-established) entity — confirm with a contabilista before printing tax on a document.
