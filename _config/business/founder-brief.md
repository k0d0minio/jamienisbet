# jamienisbet

This repo needs to be the one stop shop for running my entire business.

I'm a software engineer / AI consultant. I live in Mafra, Portugal. My one and only form of lead generation at the moment is via networking and word of mouth.

My legal business entity is not yet set up here in Portugal. That is what we need to focus on first. Advice and guidance on how to set up and reduce taxes through legal means. Workflows for proposal, quote, contract and invoice generation.

Most of my projects have their own ICM pipelines and most likely upon each new lead, a new specific pipeline gets created for that project. But we do need an extremely high level overview of any and all projects in order to understand what direction to go in, whether there are better solutions out there, and whether the project is worth my time. This process also serves as a quick way to get structured feedback for any customer on the fly without having to say "I'll get back to you on that".

I'd also like to set some processes and workflows up for marketing, sales and lead generation. I'm not too savvy on social media, and spending money on ads doesn't make sense for my business right now as I'm more of a freelancer than a full fledged corporation. I do have an idea to "employ" local friends to sell landing page websites for me. They would find the customer, sell them the idea, I make the website, the local gets 10% of anything I make. But I'm open to creative suggestions.

I'd also like to have some kind of brand identity so that my website as well as any official copy (invoicing, proposals, work orders, quotes, contracts) all have the same visual identity.

For proposal generation, I would like a hard focus on an AI that can guide me on winning negotiations in order to close the deal at a higher rate than usual. I need a system that will ask as many questions in order to gain as much information in order to give the most sound advice to close the deal.

I'd also like to host multiple websites here for my own personal use as well as some front facing client stuff, so brand and visual identity is key here too. My portfolio should exist within this structure too.

Also I'd like for this repo to act as a todo list tracker to, I can set a claude routine to run every morning and to check what still needs getting done. This will be a mix of business and personal use so make it clear that this is a standalone entity separated from the rest of business data. It will sometimes refer to it when assigned a business related task, but some stuff will definitely be personal.

If you have any other suggestions on what can be done to make this repo truly a valuable product for my business, please offer them up within a suggestions.md file.
