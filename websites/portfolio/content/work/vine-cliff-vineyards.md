## The problem

Vine Cliff Vineyards is a 170-year-old estate on the shores of Lake Erie: a
farmhouse, a carriage house and a barn, rented by the week, by the weekend, and for
events — or all at once, as the whole property.

That flexibility is the reason people book it, and it was the reason running it was
so hard. The buildings aren't independent — a barn event or a whole-estate booking
takes over the entire place. Dates lived in several places at once, because the
property was also listed on the big rental sites. And every enquiry meant a phone
call, a manual check across calendars, and a quote written by hand.

The owner also didn't want a site that simply took anyone's money automatically.
For a wedding on a heritage estate, you want to know who's coming and what they're
planning before you say yes.

## What they got

**Guests book online, the owner approves.** Each space has its own page with a live
calendar and a price that adds up as dates are chosen. Guests send a request; the
owner sees who they are and what they want, then approves, adjusts the price, or
declines. Nothing gets confirmed without a decision.

**Double-bookings became impossible.** Dates booked on the estate's own site
automatically block on the rental-site listings, and vice versa. The rule that a
barn or whole-estate booking takes the entire property is built in — those dates
simply stop being offered elsewhere.

**Guests are kept informed without anyone writing an email.** Confirmations,
approvals, changes and cancellations all go out on their own, and each guest gets
their own private page showing exactly where their booking stands.

**The whole estate runs from one screen.** Today's arrivals, the enquiry list,
every booking's status, a calendar across all three buildings, and a record of every
guest who's ever stayed — with notes. Phone bookings get typed straight in and sit
alongside the rest.

**Rates, photos and rules are the owner's to change.** Prices, minimum stays,
descriptions, house rules, photographs — all editable directly, with no developer
and no invoice for a text change.

## What it means for them

Enquiries turn into confirmed bookings without a phone call, and they arrive direct
— so there's no commission taken out of a stay that the estate found on its own.

The double-booking risk is gone, which on a wedding venue isn't an inconvenience:
it's the difference between a good year and a catastrophe.

And the work that used to fill the gaps in a week — checking calendars, writing
quotes, chasing confirmations — now happens by itself, leaving the owner to do the
part that actually makes the estate special.
