## The problem

A small firm was spending roughly half a day every week on invoice admin: copying
details between their accounting tool and their inbox, then chasing late payers by
hand. It was repetitive, easy to get wrong, and it kept pulling the owner away from
actual customer work.

## What I did

- Connected their existing tools so new invoices flowed in without manual copying.
- Automated the follow-up sequence — polite, on-brand reminders sent on a schedule,
  with a human able to step in at any point.
- Kept it plain: deterministic code for the predictable steps, AI only where it
  genuinely helped (reading messy inbound documents).

## The result

The weekly admin dropped to near zero, and follow-ups now go out on time without
anyone remembering to send them. The owner got roughly half a day a week back — and
the whole thing is documented and theirs to keep.
