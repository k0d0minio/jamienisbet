## The problem

Collabimmo handle commercial property transactions in Belgium, and they were clear
about what they didn't want: another agency site with property listings on it. Their
brief asked for one page, sober, in their brand colours, whose entire job was to get
a visitor to fill in the form and tell them as much as possible.

The more interesting problem was behind that. Their real prospecting doesn't happen
on a website at all — it happens in WhatsApp groups. That's where opportunities get
mentioned, where a lead first appears, and where it gets forgotten, because a
WhatsApp group is a conversation, not a pipeline. Good leads were scrolling past.

## What they got

**A page that does one job.** Clean, on-brand, and built entirely around the enquiry
form. Nothing on it competes with the thing they wanted visitors to do.

**Enquiries that are worth reading.** The form is protected against the automated
spam that finds any public business form within days, and submissions arrive
formatted and complete rather than as a wall of text. What lands in the inbox is
signal.

**Their WhatsApp prospecting, turned into a pipeline.** Messages posted in their
prospecting groups are read automatically and sorted into what they actually are —
a new lead, a property to note, a follow-up owed — with the next action proposed
and waiting for a yes. Nothing is filed without the owner agreeing to it.

**A view of the whole business.** Leads, deals in progress, properties, contacts,
the calendar and the numbers, in one place — so a conversation from three weeks ago
is still findable, and still moving.

## What it means for them

The website stopped being a brochure and became a way of collecting the information
they need before a first meeting.

More importantly, the deal flow that used to live in a chat thread now lives
somewhere it can be worked. A lead mentioned in passing on a Tuesday doesn't depend
on somebody remembering it on Thursday — it's already in the pipeline with a next
step against it, waiting for a decision rather than for a memory.

For an agency whose advantage is knowing about a property before it's listed, not
losing that knowledge is the entire game.
