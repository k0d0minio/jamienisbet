# Lead-Generation — Locked Configuration (v1)

> Configured 2026-06-15.

## Capacity & posture
- Selling capacity: ~5 hrs/week on lead-gen. Primary channel: **word of mouth + referrals**.
- Social media: low priority, not a focus. **Paid ads: excluded** (freelancer, not a corporation).

## The productised offer (what sellers pitch)
- **Landing page + contact form** — fixed, simple, one-sentence pitch.
- **Minimum €200.** Sellers may quote any amount ≥ €200 without approval.
- Anything more complex (multi-page, app, AI, integrations) → the seller loops in Jamie before quoting.

## Affiliate program
- **Commission: 10%**, paid on payment received from the client.
- **Attribution:** a unique referral code per seller, captured when the seller submits the lead via the
  public sellers-site (`websites/sellers-site/`).
- Sellers do **warm intros + the simple landing-page sale only**; Jamie quotes/closes anything bigger.

## Partner referrals
- Reciprocal referrals with accountants, print shops, co-working spaces, agencies — also via the
  sellers-site. Trade leads; no ad spend.

## Excluded channels
- Paid ads; heavy social-media production.

## Brand assets
- The seller starter-kit and all materials pull from `_config/brand/` (one-pager, price sheet, before/after).
