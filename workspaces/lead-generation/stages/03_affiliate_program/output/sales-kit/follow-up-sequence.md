# Follow-up Sequence — The Nudges That Close

> **ICM role:** Layer 4 — working (sales-kit). Most referrals close on the follow-up, not the opener.
> Three light touches a seller can copy and send. `{link}` is the seller's personal referral link
> (`refer.jamienisbet.com/?ref=THEIR-CODE`). Keep it warm, never pushy — one clear step each time.

## 1. Same day — after you've logged the lead
> Just sent your details over to Jamie — he'll reach out in a day or two. Anything you want him to know
> first? Tell me and I'll pass it on.

Why: closes the loop so the customer knows something's happening, and sets the expectation.

## 2. After ~3 days — if they've gone quiet
> Did Jamie manage to reach you? No rush — just making sure his message didn't land in spam. Here's his
> page again if it's easier: {link}

Why: a gentle check that's about *them*, not chasing a sale. Re-shares the link.

## 3. After ~1 week — the soft close
> No pressure at all — if now's not the right time for the website, just say and I'll leave it. If it
> is, Jamie can usually have a simple one live within a couple of weeks.

Why: gives them an easy out *and* an easy yes. Names a concrete timeframe to make "yes" feel small.

## The rule
Three touches, then stop. If they're not ready, leave the door open and check back when something
changes (a launch, a busy season). A warm "later" is worth more than a pushed "no".
