# Price Sheet — What Sellers Can Speak To

> **ICM role:** Layer 4 — working (sales-kit). Starting prices a seller can quote or reference.
> Facts from `_config/business/rates.md` and `../../../setup/output/config.md`. **Decision-support
> only** — no VAT/IVA is stated here; that depends on the entity and needs a contabilista.

## The rule of thumb
- **Landing page (€200+): quote it yourself.** No approval needed at or above €200.
- **Anything bigger: introduce Jamie.** He scopes and quotes it. The seller still earns 10%.

## Tiers

### 1. Landing page + contact form — from €200
The easy first sell. A single page that does its job.
- Loads fast, looks right on a phone
- A contact form that emails every enquiry through
- The customer's logo, colours, and words, set up properly
- Live on their domain, ready to share

**Seller:** quote this freely at €200 or more.

### 2. Multi-page site — quoted to fit
A few pages for a growing business.
- Several pages with one shared, on-brand design
- Contact form and basic enquiry handling
- Set up to be found on Google
- A short handover so they know how it works

**Seller:** make the intro — Jamie scopes and quotes. You still earn 10%.

### 3. App, booking, or automation — quoted to fit
The bigger builds, priced against the outcome at **€120/hour**.
- Built to the customer's actual workflow
- Wired into the tools they already use
- A booking system, web app, AI feature, or automation
- Ongoing support if they want it

**Seller:** just make the intro — Jamie takes the whole conversation from here. Your 10% still applies.

## What the seller never does
Quote bigger work, promise a timeline, or get into the technical detail. The job is the **warm intro**
and the **simple landing-page sale** — Jamie protects the margin and the brand by quoting the rest.
