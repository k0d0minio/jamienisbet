## The one line
A senior engineer who builds your website and looks after it — clear scope, clear price, live in weeks.

## The opening (the problem)
Most small businesses lose customers to a website that's missing, slow, or stuck in a half-finished
template. You don't need a big agency or a year-long project. You need one good page that works on a
phone, loads fast, and lets people reach you — built properly, once.

## What you get
- **A landing page + contact form — from €200.** One clean, fast page: your logo, your colours, your
  words, a form that emails you every enquiry, live on your domain.
- **A few pages if you need them** — about, services, contact — quoted to fit.
- **Something bigger** — a booking system, a web app, an AI feature, an automation wired into the tools
  you already use. Priced against the outcome, at €120/hour.

(Full tiers in [`price-sheet.md`](price-sheet.md).)

## Why work with Jamie
1. **A senior engineer, working directly with you.** No agency layers, no account managers — you talk
   to the person building it.
2. **Clear scope, clear price, no surprises.** You know what you're getting and what it costs before we
   start. Changes are a conversation, not a hidden line item.
3. **Small shop, serious delivery.** One person you can reach, with contractors on standby for bigger
   builds — so you get responsiveness *and* capacity.

## How it works
1. **We talk.** Ten minutes on what you need and who it's for.
2. **I scope and price it.** A fixed price for a landing page; a clear quote for anything bigger.
3. **I build it.** You see it come together; you don't touch the technical side.
4. **You're live — and supported.** It ships on your domain, and I'm here if it needs a change.
