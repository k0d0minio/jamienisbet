# Channel Playbooks

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (lead-generation). How to actually source leads — no paid ads.

## Word of mouth (primary)
- Make it easy to refer: a one-line description of what you do + who you help, that anyone can repeat
  (see [`referral-script-bank.md`](referral-script-bank.md)).
- After every delivery, ask for a referral and a testimonial (feeds the portfolio's case studies).

## Affiliate sellers (the 10% program)
- See [`affiliate-program.md`](affiliate-program.md). Recruit local friends; give them the starter-kit;
  they do warm intros + the simple landing-page sale.

## Partner referrals
- Accountants, print shops, co-working spaces, and web agencies serve the same customer. Set up
  reciprocal referrals via the sellers-site. Trade, don't pay.

## Networking
- Local events / communities near Mafra & Lisbon. Goal per event: one or two real conversations, not a
  stack of business cards. Follow up with the follow-up email template.

## Not doing (for now)
- Paid ads and heavy social-media production — out of scope per the founder brief.
