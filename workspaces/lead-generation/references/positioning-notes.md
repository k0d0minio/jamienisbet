# Positioning Notes — ICP & Message

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (lead-generation). The research the positioning stage draws on.

## Ideal customer (ICP)
- Small businesses and founders near Mafra / Lisbon and in Jamie's network who need a web presence or
  a practical AI/automation — reachable by word of mouth and the affiliate sellers.
- For the productised offer: any local business needing a simple landing page + contact form.

## Niche / lead offer
- Lead with the **landing page** (easy to sell, fixed scope, the affiliate wedge), then expand into
  AI features / automation / retainers for the clients worth more.

## Value proposition
- "A senior engineer who builds the thing and wires it into what you already use — clear scope, clear
  price." See [`../../../_config/brand/voice/messaging-pillars.md`](../../../_config/brand/voice/messaging-pillars.md).

## Proof points
- Real shipped work (portfolio case studies — to populate from delivered engagements).
- Direct access to the person building it; contractors on standby for scale.

## Message angles
- Local and reachable; no agency overhead; honest about build-vs-buy.
