# Referral Script Bank

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (lead-generation). Reusable phrasings for friends, affiliates, and
> partners. On-brand (friendly, plain) — see [`../../../_config/brand/voice/`](../../../_config/brand/voice/).

## What you do (one line, repeatable)
"Jamie builds websites and practical AI for small businesses — clear scope, clear price, and you deal
with him directly."

## Seller pitch (landing page)
"Need a simple website? I know someone who'll build you a clean landing page with a contact form, from
€200. Want me to introduce you?"

## Warm intro (seller → Jamie)
"Hi Jamie — [name] runs [business] and needs [a landing page / X]. I've put you both on this. My code
is [CODE]."

## Asking a happy client for a referral
"Glad that worked out. If you know anyone who needs something similar, I'd love an intro — it's how I
find most of my work."

## Partner reciprocal
"You look after their books; I build their web/AI. Want to send work each other's way when it fits?"
