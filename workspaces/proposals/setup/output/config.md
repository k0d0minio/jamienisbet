# Proposals — Locked Configuration (v1)

> Jamie's negotiation parameters. Numbers marked **(confirm)** are starting points — adjust freely;
> they anchor the whole engine. Pricing is decision-support; tax per legal-and-tax.

## Rates (EUR)
- **Standard:** €120/hour.
- **Stretch / anchor open:** ~€140–150/hour equivalent — anchor high (see negotiation-playbook). **(confirm)**
- **Floor (never below):** €90/hour — the walk-away line. **(confirm)**
- **Retainer:** committed monthly hours → ~10% reduction (≈ €108/hour). **(confirm)**
- **Commission / incentive:** open to a reduced hourly rate in exchange for outcome upside
  (revenue share, success fee, equity) — price the upside, not just the hours.

## Pricing approach
- **Tiered good / better / best by default** on every quote.
- **Value-based:** price against the client's upside where it can be estimated.

## Terms
- **Deposit:** 30–50% up front for project work. **(confirm)**
- **Payment:** net 14 days; late follow-up handled in finance. **(confirm)**
- **Currency:** EUR. Clients are foreign → IVA reverse-charged (EU) / out of scope (UK/US);
  quotes show no IVA unless the contabilista says otherwise
  (see [`../../../legal-and-tax/references/iva-vat-notes.md`](../../../legal-and-tax/references/iva-vat-notes.md)).

## Deal fit (aligns with project-triage)
- **Take:** clear or scopeable work; budget ≥ floor; software/AI consulting or a landing page.
- **Decline / redirect:** below floor with no upside; outside expertise; an off-the-shelf tool serves better.

## Coaching tone
Direct, honest, on Jamie's side. Push for the target; name the floor; say walk when it can't clear.
