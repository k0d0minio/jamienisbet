# Discovery — Question Bank & Must-Know Checklist

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (proposals). The intake brain for Stage 01.
> Bias: **ask as many questions as needed before advising.** Adaptive — keep asking until the
> must-know checklist is satisfied or each gap is explicitly flagged "unknown".

## Must-know checklist (the gate)
Stage 01 does not hand off to deal-analysis until each item is answered or explicitly flagged
unknown. Missing answers become "must ask" follow-ups in the deal dossier.

- [ ] **Who** — client, decision-maker(s), and anyone who can veto.
- [ ] **Problem** — the real underlying problem (not just the asked-for solution).
- [ ] **Why now** — what changed; the cost of doing nothing.
- [ ] **Value / upside** — what solving it is worth to them (revenue, time, risk avoided).
- [ ] **Budget** — range, who controls it, whether it's allocated.
- [ ] **Scope** — concretely what's in and what's out.
- [ ] **Timeline** — the deadline and what drives it.
- [ ] **Alternatives** — competitors, in-house, off-the-shelf, doing nothing.
- [ ] **Decision process** — how they decide, the criteria, and by when.
- [ ] **Risks / constraints** — tech, access, compliance, stakeholders.

If **budget, value, decision-maker, or alternatives** are unknown, chase those first — they drive
leverage and the number.

## Full question bank (ask adaptively, by topic)

### Client & deciders
- Who are you, and who else is involved in this decision?
- Who controls the budget? Who could say no?
- Have you bought work like this before — how did it go?

### The problem
- In your words, what's the problem you want solved?
- What have you already tried, and why didn't it stick?
- If nothing changes, what happens in six months?

### Value & upside
- If this works, what does it earn or save you? (revenue, hours, risk)
- How would you measure success — the one number that matters?
- Is this tied to a real target, or a nice-to-have?

### Budget
- What range did you have in mind?
- Is budget already set aside, or are we building the case for it?
- Is price or timing the bigger constraint?

### Scope & timeline
- What does "done" look like? What's explicitly out of scope?
- What's the deadline, and what's driving it?
- Phased, or all at once?

### Alternatives & competition
- Who else are you talking to? In-house option? An off-the-shelf tool?
- What would make you pick one option over another?

### Drivers & risk
- What's the worst outcome you're trying to avoid?
- Who needs to be comfortable for this to go ahead?
- Any constraints I should know — access, compliance, existing stack?

## Notes
Flag every unknown explicitly rather than assuming — the deal dossier must show gaps, not guesses.
Answers feed leverage-mapping in `02_deal_analysis` and the number in `05_quote`.
