## Q1 — CIRS code & coefficient: is 0.35 defensible for my work? *(the big one)*
**Ask for a yes/no *with reasons*, ideally in writing — not a shrug.**
- The 0.75 coefficient applies to activities listed in the **Art. 151 CIRS** table (engineer /
  consultant type professions); 0.35 applies to **"other services" not on that list**. The AT
  judges by **substance**, not the code typed. See [`../references/accounting-regimes.md`](../references/accounting-regimes.md).
- My concern: I present as "**consultant/engineer**," which points toward **0.75**. The 0.35 case
  looks strongest for genuine **software development / programming output** and weakest for
  advisory consultancy — and my work is a **mix**.
- **Specific questions:**
  1. For my actual deliverables (build work vs. advisory), can you **defend 0.35**, and would you
     **put that position in writing**?
  2. Does it make sense to **split by substance** — 0.35 for development work, 0.75 for advisory —
     under matched codes, rather than one blanket code?
  3. What is the **reclassification risk** (interest + penalties) if the AT reassesses to 0.75, and
     what documentation of deliverables would defend the 0.35 position?
  4. Which **CAE / CIRS codes** do you recommend registering for the above?
- **My working stance:** if you won't back 0.35 for my real mix, I register clean at 0.75 for now.
  The year-1/2 startup reduction compresses the 0.35 advantage early anyway (see Q2), so a shaky
  aggressive position isn't worth the exposure.

## Q2 — Start date: does a 1-Jul-2026 start waste the coefficient reduction?
- The **−50% yr1 / −25% yr2** coefficient reduction (Art. 31.º n.º 10) is applied per **calendar
  tax year**, not rolling 12 months. Starting mid-year means the **−50% year (2026)** only covers
  ~half a year of invoicing.
- The **12-month SS exemption** is rolling from registration, so it is **not** wasted by a mid-year start.
- **Questions:** Given I need income now (and the bar deal is live), is there any smarter start date
  within the next few weeks (e.g. a month boundary) — or is starting immediately clearly right and I
  simply accept the partial −50% window?

## Q3 — IVA on the bar (management) invoices
- Bar invoices are a **domestic B2B service** → **23% IVA** applies, unlike my foreign consulting
  work (reverse-charge / out-of-scope). See [`../references/iva-vat-notes.md`](../references/iva-vat-notes.md).
- **Questions:** correct **IVA invoice wording** for the management service; how this sits alongside
  my foreign-client invoices; anything to flag **before my first IVA filing**; confirm I register
  the **normal IVA regime** (not the franquia) for VIES.

## Q4 — >80% single-client / economic dependence (the bar)
- If the bar becomes a large share of annual invoicing, the **>80% single-client** rules and
  possible **disguised-employment** recharacterisation come into play. See
  [`../references/social-security-notes.md`](../references/social-security-notes.md).
- **Questions:** at what point does the bar trigger an **additional SS contribution** owed by the
  bar, or economic-dependence classification? What contract wording keeps it a genuine independent
  B2B service?

## Q5 — Bar structure: trial on recibos verdes, migrate to an Lda if it sticks?
- Plan: invoice the trial from the trabalhador independente; if the deal sticks and is material,
  move the bar contract into a **Unipessoal Lda** to ring-fence IVA, single-client dependence, and
  operating liability from the clean consulting activity. See [`../references/entity-structures.md`](../references/entity-structures.md).
- **Questions:** do you agree with trial-then-migrate? At what € size / duration would you form the
  Lda? Does the **owners-retain-everything** split hold up for SS/labour and licensing?
