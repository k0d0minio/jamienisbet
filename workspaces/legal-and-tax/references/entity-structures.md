# Entity Structures — Considered & Decided

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (legal & tax)
> **Decision-support only;** confirm with a contabilista. <!-- source: portugal-business-structure-analysis.md §2–4; as-of: 2026-06-15 -->

The structure decision is **made** (see [`decision-basis.md`](decision-basis.md)). This note records the options and why the chosen one wins, so the rationale stays auditable.

## Options
| Structure | Social security | Tax on extracted profit | Accounting | Liability | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Trabalhador independente, regime simplificado** ✅ | 21.4% on 70% of invoicing | IRS on the 0.75 coefficient base | no mandatory accountant | unlimited | **chosen** — lowest total tax, simplest |
| ENI (Empresário em Nome Individual) | 25.2% | as above | similar | unlimited | worse — higher SS rate, no upside here |
| Unipessoal Lda (company) | ~29.6% on director salary | 15% IRC **+ 28% dividend** on extraction | organised (~€1.5–3k/yr) | limited | worse — double tax on full extraction; forfeits startup benefits |

## The deciding factor
Everything is drawn out to live on. A company's advantage is *retaining* earnings to defer the second tax layer; pulling it all out incurs both layers. So the simplest vehicle — plain *trabalhador independente* — is also the most tax-efficient here. "Set up as a business entity" is best satisfied by this status, not an ENI or Lda.

Net-in-pocket (steady state): simplified ~€29–30k vs full-extraction Lda ~€27–28k. Numbers: [`portugal-business-structure-analysis.md`](../../../portugal-business-structure-analysis.md) §4.
