# Glossary — PT Tax & Legal Terms

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (legal & tax). <!-- as-of: 2026-06-15 -->

- **Trabalhador independente** — self-employed service provider; the chosen status.
- **Recibos verdes / fatura-recibo** — the electronic invoice-receipt issued per job via the Portal das Finanças.
- **Início de atividade** — the registration of activity with Finanças that starts self-employment.
- **Regime simplificado** — simplified tax regime; taxes a coefficient of income and deems the rest as expenses.
- **Coeficiente** — the coefficient (0.75 / 0.35) applied to gross income to get the taxable base.
- **ENI (Empresário em Nome Individual)** — sole-trader designation; higher SS (25.2%) — not chosen.
- **Unipessoal Lda** — single-shareholder limited company — not chosen.
- **IRS** — personal income tax. **IRC** — corporate income tax.
- **Segurança Social** — social security. **IAS** — the index that sets SS caps (2026 base cap €6,445.56/mo).
- **IVA** — Portuguese VAT. **VIES** — the EU system for intra-EU B2B VAT (reverse charge + recapitulative statements).
- **Derrama** — municipal surcharge on company profit (Mafra assumed ≤1.5%) — only relevant to companies.
- **Modelo 3 + Anexo B** — the annual IRS return + its self-employment annex.
- **Modelo 22 / IES** — company corporate-tax return / annual accounting declaration — not applicable to the chosen status.
- **NHR / IFICI** — tax-incentive regimes; neither held, IFICI ruled out for this profile.
- **Englobamento** — opting to aggregate certain income into the progressive IRS instead of a flat rate.
- **Contabilista certificado** — licensed Portuguese accountant; the source of truth for all of the above.
