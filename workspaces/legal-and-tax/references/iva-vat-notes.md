# IVA / VAT — Foreign-Client Services

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (legal & tax)
> **Decision-support only;** confirm with a contabilista. <!-- source: portugal-business-structure-analysis.md §7; as-of: 2026-06-15 -->

The client base is **100% non-Portuguese**, which shapes IVA handling.

- **Register for VIES** — intra-EU B2B services require the normal IVA regime + VIES registration.
- **EU business clients:** reverse-charged — invoice **without** IVA (the client accounts for it). Show both VAT numbers + the reverse-charge note.
- **UK / US (non-EU) B2B services:** outside the scope of Portuguese IVA.
- **Net effect:** little or no IVA actually charged, **but** periodic IVA declarations + recapitulative (VIES) statements must still be filed.
- This treatment is **identical** under freelancer or company — it was not a deciding factor in the structure choice.

Practical: issue **faturas-recibo (recibos verdes)** via the Portal das Finanças per invoice. Have the contabilista confirm the invoice wording (reverse-charge clause, VIES IDs) once at setup.
