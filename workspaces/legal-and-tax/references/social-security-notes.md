# Segurança Social — Self-Employed Mechanics

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (legal & tax)
> **Decision-support only;** confirm with a contabilista. <!-- source: portugal-business-structure-analysis.md §4; as-of: 2026-06-15 -->

## Rate & base
- **21.4%** applied to **70% of service invoicing** (relevant income).
- Steady state on ~€50k gross: `21.4% × 70% × €50,000 = €7,490/yr (~€624/month)`.
- Monthly base ~€2,917 is well under the 2026 cap of **€6,445.56** (12 × IAS).

## First-year exemption (the big year-1 saving)
- **Total SS exemption for the first 12 months**, automatic on first-time registration. So 2026 (year 1) SS = €0.

## Mechanics
- **Quarterly declaration** (Apr / Jul / Oct / Jan) once the 12-month exemption ends.
- Relevant income is **adjustable ±25% in 5% steps** to smooth lumpy project income.
- A **minimum €20/month** applies in any quarter declared at/near zero.

## Three-year wedge (tax + SS, pre personal credits)
| Period | Social security | Effective wedge |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 (2026) | €0 (exempt) | ~7–8% |
| Year 2 (2027) | ~€7,490 | ~27% |
| Year 3+ (2028→) | ~€7,490 | ~31–33% |

Dependent-child IRS credits (Maëlo, Amélia) reduce the IRS portion further.
