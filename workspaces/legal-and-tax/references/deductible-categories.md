# Costs & the Deemed-Expense Allowance

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (legal & tax)
> **Decision-support only;** confirm with a contabilista. <!-- source: portugal-business-structure-analysis.md §1,§3; as-of: 2026-06-15 -->

**Key point:** under the *regime simplificado* you do **not** itemise expenses to reduce the tax base — 25% is a **deemed** allowance (at the 0.75 coefficient). Real costs (~€300–400/month: SaaS + occasional office rental, ~€4k/yr ≈ 8% of gross) are **lower** than the deemed allowance, which is exactly why this regime wins here.

## So why keep receipts at all?
The **15% expense-justification rule** (see [`accounting-regimes.md`](accounting-regimes.md)) requires part of the allowance to be backed by real documented expenses — and **mandatory social-security contributions count toward it**. Keep documentation for:
- SaaS subscriptions and developer tooling
- Occasional office / co-working rental
- Hardware used for work
- Professional services (e.g. the contabilista's fee)

## What this is NOT
This is not an itemised-deduction strategy (that is the organised-accounting world this profile deliberately avoids). Don't over-engineer expense tracking to "reduce tax" — under the simplified regime, beyond the 15% justification it does not move the base.
