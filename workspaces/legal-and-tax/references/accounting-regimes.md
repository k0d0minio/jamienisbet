# The Simplified Regime — Mechanics & the Coefficient Lever

> **ICM role:** Layer 3 — reference (legal & tax)
> **Decision-support only;** confirm with a contabilista. <!-- source: portugal-business-structure-analysis.md §3,§5,§9; as-of: 2026-06-15 -->

## How the *regime simplificado* taxes income
- A **coefficient** is applied to gross service income to get the **taxable base**; the rest is a deemed-expense allowance (not itemised).
- Listed professional services (Art. 151 CIRS): **coefficient 0.75** → 75% taxable, 25% deemed expenses.
- Real costs are ~8% of gross, so ~17% of revenue is **permanently untaxed** — organised accounting can't replicate this (only real costs deductible there).
- The simplified regime only loses when real expenses exceed the 25% allowance — the opposite of this situation.

## The coefficient lever (0.75 vs 0.35) — the big open question
- **0.35** applies to "other services" not on the Art. 151 list. At 0.35 the taxable base is ~€17,500 instead of €37,500 on €50k — roughly **halving IRS**.
- Whether software development defensibly qualifies for 0.35 is a **genuine grey area**; the AT assesses the *substance* of the work, not just the CAE/activity code chosen.
- This single decision moves total tax more than freelancer-vs-company did. **Do not self-select the aggressive position blind** — have a contabilista assess specific deliverables and reclassification risk before filing início de atividade.

## Startup benefits (front-loaded; year 1 = 2026)
- **Coefficient reduction:** −50% in year 1, −25% in year 2 (Art. 31.º n.º 10 CIRS), provided no employment (Cat. A) or pension (Cat. H) income in those years — satisfied.
- Pairs with the year-1 social-security exemption — see [`social-security-notes.md`](social-security-notes.md).

## The 15% expense-justification rule
Applies to both 0.75 and 0.35 coefficients: part of the deemed allowance must be backed by real documented expenses, and **mandatory social-security contributions count toward it**. Keep invoices/receipts (see [`deductible-categories.md`](deductible-categories.md)).

## No mandatory accountant
Not legally required under the simplified regime; ~€600–1,200/yr recommended for IVA/VIES filings and the coefficient question above.
