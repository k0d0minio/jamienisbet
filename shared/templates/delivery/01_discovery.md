# 01 — Discovery: Requirements, Scope & Success Criteria

> First stage of delivery. Turn the signed proposal into a confirmed, testable scope: what we
> are building, what done looks like, and what is explicitly out of scope — before any build
> starts. This is where scope creep is fenced off and the acceptance bar for delivery is set.

## Process
Re-read the accepted proposal with the client (or the kickoff-call notes) and pin down concrete
requirements, measurable success/acceptance criteria, assumptions, dependencies, and
out-of-scope items. Produce a milestone outline and an access/dependency checklist (model/data
access, integrations, environments). Flag anything that contradicts the signed proposal.

## Outputs (write these alongside this file)
- `requirements.md`
- `success-criteria.md`
- `scope-and-assumptions.md`

## Verify
- Acceptance criteria are testable and trace back to the signed proposal.
- Scope here does not exceed (or silently shrink) the proposal; any delta is flagged for
  re-quote through the deal, not absorbed.
- Required access/dependencies are listed before build starts.

## Review gate
Scope is confirmed here before `02_build` begins. Any commercial change (rate, timeline)
routes back through the proposal/deal — never patched silently here.
