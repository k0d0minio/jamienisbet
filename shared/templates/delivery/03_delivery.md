# 03 — Delivery: Handoff, Acceptance & Retrospective

> Final stage. Package the work for handover, capture formal acceptance against the agreed
> criteria, and run a short retrospective so the next engagement gets better.

## Process
Produce the handoff package and instructions (access, docs, credentials-transfer notes).
Verify each success criterion from `01_discovery.md` is met and signed off; record the
acceptance date and any client-agreed exceptions. Write a retro (what worked, what to change).
Acceptance is the green light for final invoicing through the dashboard.

## Verify
- Every success criterion is checked off or has a documented, client-agreed exception.
- Acceptance is recorded before the final invoice is raised.
- The engagement outcome is recorded on the client in the dashboard.

## Review gate
Acceptance and the final-invoice trigger are confirmed by Jamie. Any payment terms or
commercial wording is decision-support only and must be reviewed by a licensed Portuguese
contabilista certificado / lawyer before sending where it has legal/financial weight.
