# 02 — Build: Delivery Tracking, Technical Notes & Milestones

> The middle stage. The build happens in this repo; this document keeps the coordination
> trail: technical notes, architecture/stack decisions, milestone status, and any blockers or
> scope questions that surface mid-build — so the handoff in `03_delivery.md` is reproducible.

## Process
Track the build against the confirmed scope in `01_discovery.md`. Record technical decisions
and trade-offs (for AI work: model choices, evals, integration decisions), milestone progress,
and any blockers or scope deltas. Route material changes back through the deal — anything that
changes price or timeline is a re-quote, not a quiet edit. Keep client-facing artifacts
on-brand.

## Verify
- Every success criterion from `01_discovery.md` maps to tracked build work.
- Milestones marked done are demonstrable in this repo; nothing is claimed complete that isn't.

## Review gate
Milestone status is reviewed before moving to handoff. Interim-invoiced milestones are raised
through the dashboard's get-paid step.
